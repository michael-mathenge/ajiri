# Employer-review operating plan — 26 October 2026

The showcase can be demonstrated before a real employer is onboarded. PythonAnywhere
is the host; neither payments nor a queue/storage migration blocks this scope.
Existing employer drafts, independent staff decisions, expiry and withdrawal remain
the implementation. This plan does not approve an organisation or grant staff access.

1. Identify one genuine organisation and its authorised representative through an
   independently checked organisation website/contact route. Do not rely only on
   a submitted email address or assume a mailbox proves authority.
2. General account-security mail is configured and validates in production as of
   14 November (account_security mode); retain completed owner-inbox evidence.
   Verify the genuine representative can complete current onboarding before
   relying on outside-employer delivery; configuration is not inbox acceptance.
3. An authorised account saves a factual draft: organisation, job title, location,
   requirements, official application destination and a future closing date.
   Require confirmation that applicants pay no fee to apply. No automatic publication.
4. An independently authorised staff reviewer, different from the submitting account,
   checks authority, destination, dates, credibility and applicant-fee claims. Record
   the decision, reason, actor, time and exact revision through existing controls.
   Request correction or reject unsupported claims; never fabricate evidence.
5. Before first publication, confirm public-release eligibility and test the reviewed
   vacancy and expiry boundary. Keep publication disabled until evidence passes.
   Show the official source/destination and closing date transparently.
6. Re-review substantive edits. Withdraw on early closure or credible fraud reports;
   hide expired/unverified vacancies from active browsing and alerts. Preserve audit
   records and users' existing application documents. Staff review is not a guarantee
   of employment or continuing vacancy availability.

The next owner contribution is a genuine employer contact willing to participate,
not credentials in chat. No review SLA or automated identity-verification service is
promised. Wider applicant-data sharing, real sending, retention and provider processing
still need their documented privacy/operational prerequisites. No employer is contacted
by this documentation change.

## First-employer and candidate pilot procedure

Current verified runtime is c88da83. Posting/public vacancies are off; there are
zero eligible original roles and zero employers. First milestone: one genuine
employer and one independently reviewed eligible role. The later inventory target
remains50 roles/10employers. No participants are currently confirmed.

Michael supplies a willing employer and candidate participants; this checklist
neither contacts anyone nor grants reviewer access. An existing authorised staff
reviewer must be independent of the submitting account. If no such reviewer is
available, record that dependency rather than granting access implicitly.

1. Privately record participation agreement, official organisation evidence and
   representative authority, plus independent reviewer availability. Do not place
   names, addresses, documents or account identifiers in Git/CI/Jira comments.
2. Record the genuine representative's register/verification outcome and current
   mailbox confirmation. Reuse earlier owner tests without claiming they prove
   this recipient. If delivery is uncertain, inspect the retained receipt privately;
   do not automatically retry or rewrite recipients.
3. Before enabling posting, confirm the agreed bounded intake, backup/capacity,
   verified owner and reviewer. Submit the factual draft through existing UI;
   on an uncertain submission inspect its status and reuse the same decision ID.
4. Review organisation and exact vacancy revision independently. Confirm official
   application destination, future deadline, no applicant fee and genuine authority.
   Require corrections/re-review through the existing revision controls. An expired
   or unsupported vacancy must not be approved merely to populate the pilot.
5. Enable publication only after its eligibility/release gates pass. Check canonical
   Ajiri identity, source/review/deadline labels, sitemap/JobPosting and owner/private
   exclusions. Verify expiry with synthetic tests; do not change a real deadline to
   manufacture evidence. Honour genuine early closure with recorded withdrawal.
6. Invite only separately consented candidates through Michael. Record task start,
   role-found time and outcome privately; target8/10 within five minutes. Use manual
   Android/iOS and keyboard/screen-reader checks; record device/browser and failures.
   Do not add analytics or infer consent from registration/email preferences.
7. Record the actual switch states, review/withdrawal outcome and rollback rehearsal.
   Disable publication/posting when required while retaining records, freshness,
   audits and application documents. Candidate tests do not authorise a real send.

Use [the private evidence template](PILOT_EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.md). An aggregate
`original_source_readiness` report is diagnostic only: it cannot attest identity,
inbox delivery, consent, reviewer independence or successful pilot participation.
