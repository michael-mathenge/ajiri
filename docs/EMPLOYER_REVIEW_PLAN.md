# Employer-review operating plan — 26 October 2026

The showcase can be demonstrated before a real employer is onboarded. PythonAnywhere
is the host; neither payments nor a queue/storage migration blocks this scope.
Existing employer drafts, independent staff decisions, expiry and withdrawal remain
the implementation. This plan does not approve an organisation or grant staff access.

1. Identify one genuine organisation and its authorised representative through an
   independently checked organisation website/contact route. Do not rely only on
   a submitted email address or assume a mailbox proves authority.
2. Resolve general account-verification delivery beyond the owner pilot before
   inviting an outside employer. The public guide discloses this restriction.
3. An authorised account saves a factual draft: organisation, job title, location,
   requirements, official application destination and a future closing date.
   Require confirmation that applicants pay no fee to apply. No automatic publication.
4. An independently authorised staff reviewer, different from the submitting account,
   checks authority, destination, dates, credibility and applicant-fee claims. Record
   the decision, reason, actor, time and exact revision through existing controls.
   Request correction or reject unsupported claims; never fabricate evidence.
5. Before first publication, confirm public-release eligibility and test the reviewed
   vacancy and expiry boundary. Keep publication disabled until evidence passes.
   Show the official source/destination and closing date transparently.
6. Re-review substantive edits. Withdraw on early closure or credible fraud reports;
   hide expired/unverified vacancies from active browsing and alerts. Preserve audit
   records and users' existing application documents. Staff review is not a guarantee
   of employment or continuing vacancy availability.

The next owner contribution is a genuine employer contact willing to participate,
not credentials in chat. No review SLA or automated identity-verification service is
promised. Wider applicant-data sharing, real sending, retention and provider processing
still need their documented privacy/operational prerequisites. No employer is contacted
by this documentation change.
